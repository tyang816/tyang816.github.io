> Paper: [Improved Baselines with Momentum Contrastive Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04297)

## MoCo v2: Stronger baselines via a non-linear projection head and improved data augmentation

1. Incorporates SimCLR’s MLP projection head and stronger data augmentations, together with a cosine learning-rate schedule and longer training, yielding improved results.
2. Compared with SimCLR, MoCo v2 reaches better performance in less time while using less GPU memory and smaller batch sizes.
